# First-month operating guide

GitHub Issues is the only support and feedback channel for the public preview.
Triage on two fixed days each week and reserve one maintenance day for
reproduction, fixes, and regression checks. Aim to acknowledge new reports
within three working days; do not promise a repair date before reproduction.

## Priority

- P0: duplicate execution, uncontrolled processes, or serious data problems.
  Respond immediately and withdraw recommendation of an affected release.
- P1: installation, standard MCP connection, or missing diagnostics. Repair
  before format changes or new parser coverage.
- P2: formatting, convenience, and parser additions. Rank by observed use.

Publish only verified fixes, normally no more than one routine alpha per week.
Post a weekly update listing fixes, known limitations, and missing evidence.
Every accepted parser report becomes a reviewed minimal fixture.

## Thirty-day review

Track confirmed setup, project-level real-task feedback, and explicit reuse
after at least one week. Do not treat downloads, stars, or lack of reports as
retention.

The observation targets are 10 people who confirm setup, feedback from real
tasks in 5 independent projects, and 3 people who explicitly reuse Arshy after
at least one week. These targets guide outreach and measurement; they do not
delay the preview release.

- Reuse exists: invest in frequent task paths and response-budget work.
- Setup succeeds but calls/retries are high: revise the result contract.
- Install attempts do not reach first use: revise onboarding.
- Evidence is sparse: improve the demo and distribution, then observe another cycle.
- Reuse exists without clear efficiency gains: retain the diagnostic value
  claim and continue measuring without a saving percentage.
